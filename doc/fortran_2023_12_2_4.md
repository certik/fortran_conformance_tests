### S12.2.4-003: Certain positioning or close/open actions implicitly write an endfile record after output

**Source:** S12.2.4, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF243. **Class:** Effect.

transfer for the file is output, no intervening file-positioning statement for the file
has executed, and either REWIND or BACKSPACE references the connected unit or the unit
