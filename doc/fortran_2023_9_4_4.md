### R915: A complex part designator is a designator followed by percent RE or percent IM

**Source:** 9.4.4 R915, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF153. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The syntactic category complex-part-designator is either designator followed by percent

designator category, and no additional component name, arbitrary suffix, or
intrinsic-function spelling is introduced by this rule.

### S9.4.4-001: Complex part designators select real or imaginary parts with real type and inherited kind and shape

**Source:** 9.4.4 p1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF153. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A complex-part-designator of the form designator%RE designates the real part of the
complex designator, and designator%IM designates its imaginary part. The complex part
designator has type real; its kind and shape are those of the original complex
designator, which may be an array or scalar. As an R901 designator it can participate in
broader designator or variable contexts only when the separate 9.2 and
definition-context rules allow that use; 9.4.4 itself does not turn constants or
nondefinable objects into variables.
