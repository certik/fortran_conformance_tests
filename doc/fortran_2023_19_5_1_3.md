### S19.5.1.3-001: USE statements specify use association with optional renaming and program-long effect

**Source:** S19.5.1.3, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF553. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Use association is the association of names in different scopes specified by a USE
statement. It follows 14.2.2, permits renaming, gives one scope access to entities
defined or declared in another, and remains in effect throughout program execution.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `use-stmt-name-association`, `use-renaming-cross-reference`, `use-associated-access-throughout-execution`.

storage later in another contained procedure. Distinct assertions check imported-name

persistence. Feature mutations redirect the USE to a wrong variable, remove the rename,
