### S15.4.3.1-001: Procedure interfaces are specified by procedure statements and specification statements in definitions, interface bodies, or both

**Source:** S15.4.3.1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF326. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The interface for an internal, external, module, or dummy procedure is specified by
FUNCTION, SUBROUTINE, or ENTRY statements and by specification statements for dummy
arguments and function results; these statements may appear in the procedure definition,
in an interface body, or both.
