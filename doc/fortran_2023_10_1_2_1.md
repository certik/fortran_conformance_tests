### S10.1.2.1-001: Expressions are formed from operands, operators, and parentheses

**Source:** 10.1.2.1 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p168. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The overall expression syntax is built from operands, operators, and parentheses.
Operands are scalar or array, operations are intrinsic or defined, and an operand may
itself be an expression. The detailed grammar, operator applicability, and
interpretation are owned by the following subclauses.

### S10.1.2.1-003: Expression categories correspond to precedence levels and bottom out at primary

**Source:** 10.1.2.1 p3; J3/24-007 PDF p168. **Class:** Syntax.
