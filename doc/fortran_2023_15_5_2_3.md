### S15.5.2.3-001: Conditional-argument guards are evaluated in order until the selected consequent is known

**Source:** S15.5.2.3, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF338. **Class:** Effect.

true guard is chosen, otherwise the last consequent is chosen.

### S15.5.2.3-004: Conditional-argument properties derive from all consequent arguments
