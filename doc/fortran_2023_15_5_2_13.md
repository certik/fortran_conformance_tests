### S15.5.2.13-001: Dummy argument presence and absence are defined by actual argument status

**Source:** S15.5.2.13, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF346. **Class:** Effect.

actual arguments, absent actual dummies, unallocated allocatable actuals for
