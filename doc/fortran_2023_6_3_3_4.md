### S6.3.3.4-001: Comment or line end terminates a statement only when it is not continued

**Source:** 6.3.3.4 p1; J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, PDF page 73, printed page 59, line 13. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** For a statement without continuation, either the start of commentary or the end of the
source line terminates the statement. The absence-of-continuation premise matters:
commentary after an unfinished expression does not prevent a later noncomment
continuation line from completing that expression.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `line-end`, `inline-comment`, `continued-exception`.

**Oracle:** A control uses distinct initial assignments terminated by line ends and by trailing
commentary and checks the exact integer result 7. A second control places a trailing
comment after x=1 and continues with +2 on the next noncomment line, then checks 3. No
undefined value or accidental fallthrough supplies success.

**Oracle limitation:** This is termination of statements, not program termination, INCLUDE processing, or the
execution semantics of END. The continued case is legal and must not become a fake
negative merely because there was a comment.

**Dependencies:** 6.3.3.2 defines commentary and 6.3.3.3 supplies initial/continuation status. 6.3.3.1
preserves exact fields. Ordinary assignment and addition are canonical Clause 10
context.

### S6.3.3.4-002: Semicolons separate fixed-form statements subject to context and column exceptions

**Source:** 6.3.3.4 p2 except the first-nonblank restriction; J3/24-007 PDF page 73, printed page 59, lines 14-17. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A semicolon outside character context, commentary and position 6 terminates the current
statement without becoming part of it. A following statement can begin on that same line
and can itself continue on later lines. Any sequence containing only blanks and one or
more such semicolons is equivalent to one terminator; it does not introduce extra
executable statements. Semicolons in either character context, in commentary, or in the
continuation position retain their other meanings.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `same-line-next`, `same-line-continued`, `blank-semicolon-runs`, `trailing-runs`, `literal-exception`, `edit-descriptor-exception`, `comment-exception`, `column-six-exception`.

**Oracle:** One program executes successive assignments separated by single semicolons, runs of
semicolons with and without blanks, and trailing runs, checking independently computed
values 7 and 10. A second begins x=1 after a terminating semicolon and completes it with
+2 on a continuation line. A third checks literal and character-edit-descriptor
semicolons as exact character data, ignores statement-looking text after a comment, and
checks that a semicolon in column 6 continues arithmetic rather than terminating it.

**Oracle limitation:** Only real terminators participate in the blank/semicolon equivalence; quoted or
commented semicolons do not. Same-line labels are excluded by 6.3.3.5 and remain owned
by S6.2.5-005. This effect catalogue does not reallocate that diagnostic rule or weaken
the separate initial-line restriction.

**Dependencies:** 3.21 defines character context; R724 and 13.2.1/13.3.2 supply its two forms.
6.3.3.2/6.3.3.3 distinguish commentary and position 6. 6.2.5 p3 and 6.3.3.5 p1 retain
the same-line label exclusion.

### S6.3.3.4-003: An initial line cannot begin with a nonblank semicolon

**Source:** 6.3.3.4 p2, fourth sentence; J3/24-007 PDF page 73, printed page 59, line 16. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** A semicolon cannot be the first nonblank character of an initial line. Optional leading
blanks do not waive the restriction, and the permission for a run of terminators is not
permission for an initial empty statement. An indicator semicolon in position 6 and a
semicolon already inside commentary are different cases.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `semicolon-in-column-seven`, `semicolon-after-statement-field-blanks`.

**Oracle:** Two byte-preserved diagnose fixtures put the offending semicolon in column 7 or column
12 of an otherwise unlabeled initial assignment line. Replacing only that semicolon by a
blank yields the paired run control, whose initialized scalar must become 3. Require a
diagnostic at that exact source line; nonfatal reports are allowed only by explicitly
matched compiler-family, severity and source-specific message predicates.

**Oracle limitation:** The line has blank label and continuation fields and an otherwise legal assignment. No
second defect involving a label, omitted operand, or orphan continuation is used to
obtain a report. A located ordinary error suffices irrespective of exit status; crashes,
timeouts, earlier-step failures and unrelated or unlocated reports do not.

**Dependencies:** 6.3.3.3 p1 defines an initial line, 6.3.3.5 p1 defines its label field, and 4.2 p2(5),
PDF page 46, supplies the Clause 6 reporting capability without mandating rejection.
