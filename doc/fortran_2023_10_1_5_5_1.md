## Relational intrinsic operation runtime observations

Eleven complete run/effect/f2023 programs cover twelve selected facets. Six integer fixtures evaluate each dotted relational spelling and its symbolic spelling on identical operands, with paired less/equal/greater ordering rows and .NEQV. sentinels proving the two spellings agree. Each operator's row set is inconsistent with every one of the other five relational operators, and wrong-operator substitution plans permanently cover all thirty operator/alternative pairs for both spellings.
