### S17.9-001: IEEE_SUPPORT_DATATYPE true entails the listed IEEE arithmetic facilities

**Source:** S17.9, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF484. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** IEEE_SUPPORT_DATATYPE inquires support for IEEE arithmetic for a real kind; for a
supported kind, normal numbers match an ISO/IEC/IEEE 60559 format, exact normal
addition, subtraction, and multiplication conform for at least one rounding mode, ABS
provides abs, IEEE_REM provides remainder, and IEEE_COPY_SIGN, IEEE_LOGB, and
IEEE_UNORDERED provide copySign, logB, and compareQuietUnordered.
