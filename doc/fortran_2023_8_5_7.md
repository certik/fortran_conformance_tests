### S8.5.7-003: The seven stated routes guarantee actual contiguity

**Source:** 8.5.7 p2 and list, physical PDF123, lines20-38 **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Contiguity is guaranteed for an object with CONTIGUOUS; a nonpointer whole array that is
