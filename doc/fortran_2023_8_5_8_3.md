An assumed-shape array is a nonallocatable nonpointer dummy array.
The scalar-list and vector-bounds productions are distinct; R740 component
grammar is not expanded by these ordinary-entity forms. A zero-size lower
vector establishes a scalar, not a zero-extent rank-one array. RANK clauses
retain their own defining rules and eligible entity roles.

The dummy receives corresponding extents, not every actual lower bound.
Its declared lower bounds come from scalar/vector specifications or one.
The upper-bound relation and an inquiry's result are different in empty
dimensions: for shape `[0,4]` and dummy lowers `[5,-2]`, whole-dummy
LBOUND/UBOUND give `[1,-2]` and `[0,1]`. Zero total size does not normalize
the second dimension. No element of the empty array is accessed.

**Source:** 8.5.8.3 p3, physical PDF126, lines4-5 **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A present explicit-bounds-expr supplies lower bounds for every dimension. Otherwise each

### S8.5.8.3-003: Effective-argument extents and the dummy lower bound determine the upper bound

**Source:** 8.5.8.3 p4, physical PDF126, lines6-8 **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Each dimension of an assumed-shape dummy has the extent of the corresponding
