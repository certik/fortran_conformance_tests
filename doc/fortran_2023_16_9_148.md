### S16.9.148-003: MVBITS copies selected bits and leaves all other TO bits unaltered

**Source:** S16.9.148, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF438. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** MVBITS defines TO by copying LEN bits starting at FROMPOS of FROM to TOPOS of TO; no
other TO bits are altered, and the copied TO bits equal the entry value of the selected
FROM bits on return.
