### S10.1.6.3-001: A processor may evaluate an equivalent expression for an established defined operation if parentheses integrity is preserved

**Source:** 10.1.6.3 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p182. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Once the interpretation of a defined operation is established, the processor has
latitude to evaluate another equivalent expression, but only when the integrity of
parentheses is not violated.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `established-interpretation-precondition`, `equivalent-expression-evaluation-latitude`, `parentheses-integrity-not-violated`, `no-processor-strategy-oracle`.

**Oracle:** This is a processor permission, not a required behavior. No future plan may assert that
a processor did or did not exercise the latitude; plans may only avoid false oracles and
test requirements that remain invariant under all permitted equivalent evaluations.

**Oracle limitation:** This source-only catalogue records pending plans only. It creates no Fortran test
program, invokes no processor, approves no fixture or oracle, and claims no coverage.
Future cases must not infer which operand was evaluated, the order of evaluation, or
whether an optional evaluation was omitted; must not assert a result that a permitted
mathematically or logically equivalent rewrite could change; must not use floating-point
tolerance as an escape hatch; must not rely on compiler agreement, address arithmetic,
TRANSFER, emitted assembly, or undefined reads; and must attribute every negative to the
cited requirement rather than to an independently invalid rule owned elsewhere.

### S10.1.6.3-002: Derived-type expression equivalence requires equal values for all possible primary values

**Source:** 10.1.6.3 p2; J3/24-007 PDF p182. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** For expressions of derived type, equivalence means that the expressions have equal
values for every possible assignment of values to their primaries. A difference for any
possible primary values establishes non-equivalence.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `derived-type-expression-equivalence`, `equal-values-for-all-primaries`, `sampled-values-not-a-proof`, `nonassociative-defined-operation-non-equivalence`.

**Oracle:** Future controls may use a non-associative defined operation to refute equivalence by
producing two parenthesised expressions with independently computed unequal values. They
must not claim universal equivalence from finite compiler executions.

program, invokes no processor, approves no fixture or oracle, and claims no coverage.

TRANSFER, emitted assembly, or undefined reads; and must attribute every negative to the
