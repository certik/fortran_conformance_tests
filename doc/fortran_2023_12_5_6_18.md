### `sign_value_tokens` / `S12.5.6.18-001`

**Facets:** `sign-value-set`.

12.5.6.18 p1 lists PLUS and SUPPRESS as SIGN values; INQUIRE(SIGN=) returns their fixed tokens.

Feature mutations: `sign-plus-token-to-suppress`, `sign-suppress-token-to-plus`.

### `sign_mode_effect` / `S12.5.6.18-003`

**Facets:** `sign-mode-effect`.

12.5.6.18 p1 sets the connection sign mode; SIGN='PLUS' emits +7 while SIGN='SUPPRESS' emits blank-plus suppression for positive I output.

Feature mutations: `sign-plus-output-to-suppress`, `sign-suppress-output-to-plus`.

### `sign_changeable_mode` / `S12.5.6.18-004`

**Facets:** `sign-changeable-mode`.

12.5.6.18 p1 says SIGN is changeable; same-unit OPEN changes SUPPRESS to PLUS and exact I output shows the plus sign.

Feature mutations: `sign-changeable-keeps-suppress`.

### `sign_default_processor_defined` / `S12.5.6.18-005`

**Facets:** `sign-default-token-processor-defined`.

12.5.6.18 p1 makes omitted SIGN default to PROCESSOR_DEFINED for an initiating formatted OPEN; INQUIRE observes that token.

Feature mutations: `sign-default-made-plus`.
