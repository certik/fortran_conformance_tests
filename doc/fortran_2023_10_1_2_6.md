### S10.1.2.6-001: Level-3 expressions are the character concatenation layer over level-2 expressions

**Source:** 10.1.2.6 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p170. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** Level-3 expressions are level-2 expressions optionally involving concat-op. This places
concatenation above relational and logical operations but below numeric level-2 syntax
in the grammar ladder.

### R1011: A level-3 expression is a left-recursive chain of level-2 expressions joined by concat-op

**Source:** 10.1.2.6 R1011; J3/24-007 PDF p170. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The level-3-expr syntax is either a level-2-expr or a level-3-expr followed by concat-op
and level-2-expr, making unparenthesized concatenation left-recursive in the grammar.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

### R1012: The concatenation operator token is double slash

**Source:** 10.1.2.6 R1012; J3/24-007 PDF p170. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The concat-op token is '//'. It is the token used by R1011 and does not define character
operand validity by itself.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `double-slash-token`, `single-slash-not-concat`.
