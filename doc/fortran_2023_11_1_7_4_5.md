### S11.1.7.4.5-001: A non-DO-CONCURRENT loop terminates and becomes inactive on its specified termination events

**Source:** 11.1.7.4.5 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p214. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** For a DO construct that is not DO CONCURRENT, the loop terminates and the DO construct
becomes inactive when the iteration count is zero or the WHILE expression is false
during execution-cycle step (1), an EXIT statement belonging to the DO construct is
executed, an EXIT or CYCLE statement belonging to an outer construct and within the DO
construct is executed, a branch within the DO construct targets outside the construct,
or a RETURN statement within the DO construct is executed.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `count-or-while-termination`, `owned-exit-terminates-loop`, `outer-exit-or-cycle-terminates-inner-loop`, `branch-outside-terminates-loop`, `return-terminates-loop`.
