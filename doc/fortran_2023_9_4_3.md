### S9.4.3-001: Coindexed named objects are named scalar coarray variables selected by an image selector

**Source:** 9.4.3 p1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF153. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A coindexed-named-object is formed from a named scalar coarray variable followed by an
image selector. The coarray object's corank, cobounds, image index, team, STAT, NOTIFY,
TEAM and TEAM_NUMBER details are not defined here; they remain owned by the
data-ref/part-ref and image-selector rules in 9.4.2 and 9.6.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

### R914: A coindexed named object has data-ref syntax

**Source:** 9.4.3 R914, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF153. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The syntactic form coindexed-named-object is data-ref. C921 then narrows that data-ref
to one part-ref containing an image selector whose part-name is a scalar coarray name;
arbitrary data-ref chains are not admitted by R914 alone as conforming coindexed named

### C921: A coindexed named object has exactly one image-selected scalar coarray part

**Source:** 9.4.3 C921, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF153. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** For an R914 coindexed-named-object, the data-ref shall contain exactly one part-ref.
That part-ref shall contain an image-selector, and its part-name shall be the name of a
scalar coarray. The image-selector's cosubscripts, optional specifiers, team and
image-index validity are dependencies from 9.6 rather than separate 9.4.3 ownership.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.
