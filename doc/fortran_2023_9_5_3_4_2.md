### S9.5.3.4.2-001: A subscript triplet designates a regular sequence with optional endpoints and stride defaults

**Source:** 9.5.3.4.2 p1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF157. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A subscript triplet designates a regular sequence of zero or more subscript values. Its
stride specifies the increment between values. The first subscript, second subscript,
and stride may be omitted; omitted first and second subscripts are equivalent to the
lower and upper bounds for the array dimension, and an omitted stride is equivalent to
stride 1.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `regular-sequence-zero-or-more-values`, `stride-is-increment`, `first-subscript-defaults-to-lower-bound`, `second-subscript-defaults-to-upper-bound`, `omitted-stride-defaults-to-one`.

### S9.5.3.4.2-003: Positive strides proceed upward through the largest value not greater than the second subscript

**Source:** 9.5.3.4.2 p3, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF157. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** When the stride is positive, the triplet sequence begins with the first subscript and
proceeds by that positive increment to the largest generated integer not greater than
the second subscript. The sequence is empty if the first subscript is greater than the
