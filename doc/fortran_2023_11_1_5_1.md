### S11.1.5.1-001: CHANGE TEAM changes the current team and admits named construct coarray association

**Source:** 11.1.5.1 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p207. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The CHANGE TEAM construct is the execution-control construct that changes the current
team. Named construct entities can be associated with coarrays in the containing scoping
unit in the same way as for the ASSOCIATE construct; the association machinery and
named-entity details are dependencies rather than redefined here.
