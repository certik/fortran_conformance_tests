### S11.1.7.4.3-001: A non-DO-CONCURRENT execution cycle tests for termination, executes the block, then decrements the count and increments the DO variable

**Source:** 11.1.7.4.3 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p213. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The execution cycle of a DO construct that is not DO CONCURRENT repeatedly performs, in
sequence, testing the iteration count and WHILE expression for termination, executing
the loop block, and then decrementing the iteration count and incrementing the DO
