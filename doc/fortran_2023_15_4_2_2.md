### S15.4.2.2-001: Non-statement-function procedure identifiers require explicit interfaces in the listed contexts

**Source:** S15.4.2.2, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF325. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** Within the scope of a procedure identifier, a non-statement-function procedure is
required to have an explicit interface for keyword arguments, pure or simple required
contexts, the listed dummy argument properties, the listed result properties, elemental
