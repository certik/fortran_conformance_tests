## DO construct form fixture packet

Twenty facets are discharged by complete f2023 fixtures. Seventeen positive
admission cases execute exact sentinel-backed traces for nonlabel DO, label DO,
no-loop-control DO, labeled CONTINUE end-do, and selected DO CONCURRENT header
and locality forms. Three C1135 cases cover one matching construct-name control
and two one-property END DO name diagnostics. Label-DO fixtures place an
after-label statement outside the labeled range and require it to execute once.

The packet intentionally does not migrate C1121 or R1123, does not cover branch
target facets, and does not claim 11.1.7.4 loop execution semantics such as
iteration-count definition or final DO-variable value. Masked DO CONCURRENT and
END-name mismatch probes remain pending because the frozen target does not yet
provide clean reference-compatible validation for those forms.
