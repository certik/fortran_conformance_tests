An external procedure, a dummy procedure, a procedure pointer and block
data are distinct categories. Neither a role declaration nor the existence
of an EXTERNAL attribute proves pointer association, a callable target,
linkage or an explicit interface. Block data is not a procedure.

The own-procedure prohibitions in C843/C844 are scope-specific. An interface
body may supply EXTERNAL in its containing context while forbidding an
inner specification for its own declared procedure. Module and internal
procedure arguments are not made external by the operations listed in p2.

The p2 declaration obligation is retained, but a mandatory diagnostic is
not inferred from the word "shall" alone. Any diagnosis must have the exact
numbered/scope reporting basis or an explicit compiler-policy owner.
Admissible callback and pointer-target programs are positive controls,
not fabricated new procedure-association effects.

### C842: One entity cannot have both EXTERNAL and INTRINSIC

**Source:** 8.5.9 C842, physical PDF128, printed114, line12 **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** EXTERNAL and INTRINSIC cannot both describe the same entity. This does not prohibit two
different entities from having the respective attributes, or an appropriately declared
external procedure from using the spelling of an intrinsic procedure.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `attribute-conflict`, `declaration-order`, `separate-role-controls`, `intrinsic-shadowing-source`.

**Oracle:** A same-entity attribute conflict with one-attribute repairs and separate-role controls.
Bind cause, declared source identity and actual allowed diagnostic locations.

**Oracle limitation:** 4.2p2(3) requires reporting capability, not fatal rejection, a particular English
sentence or a printed constraint code. Unsupported intrinsic facilities, unrelated
name/type recovery, source echoes and native compiler failures cannot satisfy the
predicate.

**Dependencies:** 8.5.11;8.2;15.4.3.5/.6;16.2 and specific-intrinsic eligibility;19.3;4.2p2.

### C843: An external subprogram cannot specify EXTERNAL for its own defined procedure

**Source:** 8.5.9 C843, physical PDF128, lines13-14 **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** Within an external subprogram, a procedure defined by that subprogram is not given an
EXTERNAL specification there. This is a restriction on the defining scope, not a ban on
declaring the procedure EXTERNAL in a caller or on declaring other external procedures
in the subprogram.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `subroutine-own-name`, `function-own-name`, `other-procedure-control`, `entry-scope-source`.

**Oracle:** Own-procedure declaration negatives with complete one-statement repairs and contrasting
external use sites.

**Oracle limitation:** Required diagnostic capability does not prescribe fatal status, text or code. A parser
rejection of ENTRY, a result-variable error, unrelated scope conflict or recursion
limitation is not causal evidence of C843.

**Dependencies:** 15.6.2.2/.3/.6;15.4.3.5/.6;19.3.1p4;19.3.3;4.2p2.

### C844: An interface body cannot specify EXTERNAL for its own declared procedure

**Source:** 8.5.9 C844, physical PDF128, lines15-16 **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** The procedure declared by an interface body cannot be given an EXTERNAL specification
within that body. The interface body can itself supply EXTERNAL in its containing
context under15.4.3.2p5; that is a different declaration relationship.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `subroutine-interface-own-name`, `function-interface-own-name`, `declaration-source-relations`, `other-interface-categories-source`.

**Oracle:** Inner-own-procedure specification negatives, minimal repairs and source-qualified
containing-scope attribute relations.

**Oracle limitation:** A wrong-scope import, duplicate explicit interface, unsupported separate-module facility
or unrelated result declaration is not C844 evidence. Reporting capability has no
fatal-exit, exact-English or printed-code requirement.

**Dependencies:** 15.4.3.2p2-p7;15.4.3.5/.6;19.3.1;4.2p2.

### S8.5.9-001: External and dummy procedure arguments or pointer targets require an EXTERNAL declaration

**Source:** 8.5.9 p2, physical PDF128, lines17-18 **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** An external procedure or dummy procedure used as an actual argument or as the target of
procedure pointer assignment must be declared EXTERNAL. A qualifying interface body or
procedure declaration can already provide the attribute; a separate EXTERNAL statement
is not universally required. The rule does not reclassify internal or module procedures
as external.

**Diagnostic obligation:** context-dependent.

**Facets:** `external-as-actual`, `dummy-as-actual`, `external-as-pointer-target`, `dummy-as-pointer-target`, `declaration-mechanism-census`, `module-internal-boundaries`, `missing-declaration-reporting-gate`.

**Oracle:** Complete declared-procedure positive controls and finite canonical declaration/use
relations. Any callback result has independent literal expectations, valid
characteristics and live association; the controls demonstrate only their finite
admissible contexts.

**Oracle limitation:** No compulsory diagnostic is inferred solely from a prose restriction. Existing
validators require a separate policy basis for an S-level invalid case without a
required standard diagnostic. Source/role gaps stay pending, never bypassed by retagging
effects or inventing canonical approval.

**Dependencies:** 8.2;15.4.3.2p5/p7;15.4.3.5;15.4.3.6p1-p10;15.5.2.10p1-p7;10.2.2.4;19.3 and19.5;4.2p2.
