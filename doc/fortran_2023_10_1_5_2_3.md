### S10.1.5.2.3-001: Complex raised to complex power has the principal value

**Source:** 10.1.5.2.3 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p177. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** For a complex value raised to a complex power, the value of x1 ** x2 is the principal
value of x1 raised to x2.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `complex-one-to-complex-power`, `complex-base-complex-one-power`, `principal-branch-boundary`, `nonportable-approximation-boundary`.
