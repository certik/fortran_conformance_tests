### S9.7.3.1-001: DEALLOCATE deallocates allocatable variables and pointer targets

**Source:** 9.7.3.1 p1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF164. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Execution of a DEALLOCATE statement deallocates the listed allocatable variables. When a
listed allocate-object is a pointer, execution deallocates the pointer target and causes
the listed pointer to become disassociated; the requirements that make an allocatable
variable or pointer target eligible for deallocation are owned by 9.7.3.2 and 9.7.3.3,
and the STAT-determined status after an error is owned by 9.7.4.

### S9.7.3.1-002: Allocate objects in the same DEALLOCATE statement are independent of each other and options
