### S11.1.12-003: Executing EXIT terminates the construct it belongs to and handles CHANGE TEAM as END TEAM transfer

**Source:** 11.1.12 p3; J3/24-007 PDF p227. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** When EXIT belongs to a DO construct, it terminates that loop and active loops contained
within it. When EXIT belongs to a non-DO construct, it terminates active loops contained
within that construct and completes execution of that construct. If it belongs to CHANGE
TEAM, the effect is the same as transferring control to END TEAM, with STAT= or ERRMSG=
variables defined as specified for END TEAM when present.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.
