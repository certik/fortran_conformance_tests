# 6.3.2.1 Free form line length

Draft catalogue: `doc/catalogues/free_form.json`. Source: checksum-pinned
J3/24-007 (18 December 2023), PDF page 71 / printed page 57. Its limit is
**10000 characters per physical line**, not the earlier 132-character limit.
The one-million-character statement limit belongs to 6.3.2.6 and is not authored
here.

The `free_form_*` raw manifests preserve every input byte, including trailing
blanks. Their sources contain no inline metadata or diagnostic markers. ASCII
characters and bytes are counted without the LF terminator; a blank physical
line has zero characters. The paired 10000/10001-character content, trailing-blank,
and comment inputs differ by one character. Additional controls place an entire
statement late on a 10000-character line and end a line at column 133.

Compiler observations are draft evidence, not independent fixture approval.
No line-length override or capability-profile skip changes the F2023 oracle.

### S6.3.2.1-001: A free-form physical line has at most 10000 characters

**Source:** 6.3.2.1 p1, third sentence; J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, PDF page 71, printed page 57, lines 12-13. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** A free-form source line is limited to 10000 characters, including leading blanks,
trailing blanks, and any comment text. The limit is on each physical line, not on an
entire continued statement. Empty lines and placement of statements anywhere within a
line are permitted by the other sentences of this paragraph.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `content-at-limit`, `content-over-limit`, `trailing-blanks-at-limit`, `trailing-blanks-over-limit`, `comment-at-limit`, `comment-over-limit`, `late-statement-at-limit`, `past-column-132`.

**Oracle:** Eight out-of-band fixtures preserve ASCII input bytes and LF physical-line boundaries.
Three paired shapes have a distinguished line of exactly 10000 or 10001 characters: a
character-literal assignment whose final quote reaches the boundary, an ordinary
assignment padded with trailing blanks, and a comment. At-limit controls compile, link,
and run; over-limit cases require a source-located compile diagnostic on that line. Each
invalid asset differs from its conforming repair by exactly one source character. A
fourth 10000-character control starts its assignment after 9990 leading blanks. A
separate 133-character control distinguishes the older configured 132-column limit
without weakening the F2023 oracle. Every run control checks defined data; the
long-literal control checks all 9990 x characters, the final Z, and blank padding.

**Oracle limitation:** These are byte-preserved ASCII witnesses: character and byte counts agree by
construction. They do not establish character-count handling for multibyte encodings or
the separate one-million-character statement limit in 6.3.2.6. No annotations,
permissive line-length flags, or optional capability profiles are added. An older
reference default that truncates at column 132 is configuration evidence, not a reason
to reject the pinned F2023 premise or skip the case. 4.2 p2(5) requires reporting
capability, not compulsory fatal rejection; nonfatal diagnostics unsupported by the
existing runner remain a reporting-adapter limitation. A compiler crash or timeout never
satisfies a negative case.
