### S5.4.9-001: Nonassociated pointers are not referenced or defined

**Source:** 5.4.9 p2, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF64. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** A pointer whose association status is not associated is outside the conforming source
boundary for reference or definition. This restriction is about pointer association
status, not target value, declaration syntax or target lifetime.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `associated-reference-control`, `associated-definition-control`, `nonassociated-source-boundary`.

**Oracle:** Positive controls establish associated status before every reference or definition and
use distinct integer sentinels. The nonassociated variant is a source-boundary contrast
only.

**Oracle limitation:** Executing a nonassociated pointer reference or definition would be a nonconforming
program, and this unnumbered shall does not require a processor diagnostic. Crashes,
traps, stale target values and diagnostic text are not portable evidence.

**Dependencies:** 19.5.2.2 defines association status; 10.2.2.3 and 10.2.2.4 define pointer assignment
effects; 16.9 ASSOCIATED can observe defined association status; target lifetime rules
remain with Clause 19 and procedure association catalogues.
