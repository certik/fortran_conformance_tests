### S10.1.5.2.2-001: Integer division yields the integer closest to and between zero and the mathematical quotient

**Source:** 10.1.5.2.2 p1; J3/24-007 PDF pp176-177. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** When one integer operand is divided by another integer operand using /, Table 10.2 makes
the expression type integer. The result is the integer closest to the mathematical
quotient and between zero and that mathematical quotient, inclusively.
