Both base units and six fine units are accounted as the abstract definition
and its four characteristics. There is no local executable requirement.
Types, kind identifiers, storage representations, character codes and ranks
must not be conflated. A finite set of initialized objects cannot establish
every type's domain or operation set.

The actual accessibility, operation and parameter obligations are retained
in the neighboring catalogues, including explicit pending source/use-graph
and processor-interface evidence. Definition accounting is neither a
compiler pass nor independent source-review approval.
