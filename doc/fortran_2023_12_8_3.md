## WAIT/position/FLUSH fixture derivations

### `endfile_sequential_record` / `S12.8.3-001`

**Facets:** `sequential-endfile-writes-endfile-record`, `sequential-endfile-record-becomes-last`.

12.8.3 p1 writes an endfile record as the next record and makes it last; one scenario observes the endfile after A/B, and one writes ENDFILE before prior trailing records E/F and observes D followed by IOSTAT_END.

Feature mutations: `replace-endfile-with-data-record`, `remove-last-record-endfile`.

### `endfile_position_after` / `S12.8.3-001`

**Facets:** `sequential-endfile-positions-after-endfile`.

12.8.3 p1 positions after the endfile record; the required BACKSPACE before a following transfer therefore positions before the endfile record, and the next READ reports IOSTAT_END.

Feature mutations: `remove-endfile-before-backspace`, `replace-backspace-with-rewind`.

### `stream_endfile_terminal` / `S12.8.3-004`

**Facets:** `stream-endfile-terminal-point-current-position`, `stream-endfile-only-before-current-written`, `stream-endfile-only-before-current-read`.

12.8.3 p3 sets a stream endfile terminal point to the current POS= position; positions before it read exact bytes A and X, while reading at the terminal point reports IOSTAT_END.

Feature mutations: `remove-stream-endfile`, `replace-stream-endfile-with-flush`, `replace-stream-endfile-with-rewind`.
