### S8.10.1.3-001: Default CHARACTER alignment permits unequal lengths

**Source:** 8.10.1.3 p1, p2, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF147 **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Default CHARACTER objects may share storage only with default CHARACTER or
character-SEQUENCE objects, as already constrained by C8115. Their lengths need not
match. Nonempty default-character sequences align their first character units; empty

sequences. Containing objects can consequently overlap. Only characters whose definition
state is established may be referenced.
