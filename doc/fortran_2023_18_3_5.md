### S18.3.5-001: Named scalar Fortran variables are interoperable under scalar eligibility conditions

**Source:** S18.3.5, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF527. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A named scalar Fortran variable is interoperable exactly with interoperable type and
type parameters, non-coarray status, absence of ALLOCATABLE and POINTER, and constant
nonassumed character length for character variables.
