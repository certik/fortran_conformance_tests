### S9.4.1-001: Substring value, defaults and length follow the effective starting and ending points

**Source:** 9.4.1 p1-p3, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF151. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A substring is a contiguous portion of a character string. The first scalar-int-expr
supplies the starting point f and the second supplies the ending point l; omitted f
defaults to 1 and omitted l defaults to the parent string length n. Parent characters
are numbered from 1 through n, and the substring contains the characters from f through
l inclusive in sequence. Its length is MAX(l - f + 1, 0), so f greater than l yields a
zero-length substring.
