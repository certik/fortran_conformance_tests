### S11.7.1-001: Image control statements divide an image execution sequence into segments and include the listed statement forms

**Source:** 11.7.1 p2; J3/24-007 PDF p230. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Execution of an image control statement divides the execution sequence on an image into
segments. The listed statement classes are image control statements, including SYNC ALL,
SYNC IMAGES, SYNC MEMORY, SYNC TEAM, selected coarray allocation and deallocation
statements, CHANGE TEAM and END TEAM, CRITICAL and END CRITICAL, event, team, lock,
MOVE_ALLOC-with-coarray, STOP, and main-program END forms.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `image-control-statement-divides-segments`, `sync-statements-classified-image-control`, `coarray-allocation-deallocation-classified-image-control`, `team-critical-event-lock-stop-end-classified-image-control`.

**Oracle:** This requirement owns the 11.7.1 classification and segment-boundary consequence only.
It does not approve a program that tries to observe scheduling, waiting, or cross-image
ordering from executing the listed statements.

**Oracle limitation:** This source-only catalogue records pending plans only. It creates no Fortran test
program, invokes no processor, approves no fixture or oracle, and claims no coverage.
Future fixtures must use independently derived source relations or portable single-image
observations when such observations exist; they must not infer synchronization, segment
ordering, scheduling, progress, failure, or waiting from timing, hangs, races,
interleaving, or vacuous single-image execution; and they must not rely on diagnostic
wording, compiler agreement, processor-dependent STAT or ERRMSG values, manufactured
stopped or failed images, resource exhaustion, or unregistered multi-image
infrastructure.

**Dependencies:** 5.3.5 execution sequence NOT YET REGISTERED; 9.7 allocation/deallocation registered;
11.1.5 CHANGE TEAM and 11.1.6 CRITICAL registered; 11.7.3-11.7.6 in this packet;
11.7.7-11.7.11 NOT YET REGISTERED; Clauses 12-19 NOT YET REGISTERED for STOP, END,
MOVE_ALLOC, teams, events, locks, and ISO_FORTRAN_ENV.

### S11.7.1-002: At most one procedure invocation in a statement may execute an image control statement other than CRITICAL or END CRITICAL

**Source:** 11.7.1 p3; J3/24-007 PDF p230. **Class:** Restriction.
