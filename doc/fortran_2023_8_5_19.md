### C868: VALUE excludes allocation, pointer, output-intent and volatility attributes

**Source:** 8.5.19 C868, physical PDF133, printed119 **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** A VALUE entity has none of ALLOCATABLE, POINTER, VOLATILE, INTENT(OUT) or INTENT(INOUT).
The restriction is on that dummy entity: pointer or allocatable components and
corresponding actual-argument attributes are not automatically the same predicate.
INTENT(IN), absence of INTENT, and TARGET are not excluded by this list, but retain all
other rules.

### C869: A BIND procedure dummy cannot combine OPTIONAL and VALUE

**Source:** 8.5.19 C869, physical PDF133, printed119 **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** When a procedure has BIND, none of its dummy arguments has both OPTIONAL and VALUE. The
conjunction is conditional on the procedure's attribute: it neither bans each attribute
separately for all interoperable dummies nor prohibits the combination in every non-BIND
procedure. Interoperability, type, rank, characteristics and presence have separate
canonical conditions.
