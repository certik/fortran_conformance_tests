## WAIT/position/FLUSH fixture derivations

### `flush_forms` / `R1228`

**Facets:** `flush-unit-number-form`, `flush-spec-list-form`.

R1228 gives both FLUSH unit-number and parenthesized flush-spec-list forms; the spec-list form on a connected file reports IOSTAT zero, and no external visibility is asserted.

Feature mutations: `flush-unit-form-wrong-positioner`, `remove-flush-spec-form`.

### `flush_specifiers` / `R1229`

**Facets:** `flush-iostat-specifier-form`, `flush-iomsg-specifier-form`, `flush-err-label-specifier-form`.

R1229 admits IOSTAT, IOMSG, and ERR flush specifiers; successful FLUSH gives IOSTAT zero and no ERR branch, with IOMSG text left unspecified and untested.

Feature mutations: `flush-remove-iostat-statement`, `flush-iomsg-as-iostat-probe`, `flush-err-label-target`.

### `flush_position_unchanged` / `S12.9-005`

**Facets:** `flush-file-position-unchanged`.

12.9 p3 says FLUSH has no effect on file position; after reading A, FLUSH leaves the next sequential read positioned at B.

Feature mutations: `flush-position-rewind-mutant`, `flush-position-second-record-changed`.

### `flush_duplicate_iostat` / `C1244`

**Facets:** `flush-duplicate-specifier-rejected`.

C1244 prohibits duplicate FLUSH specifiers; the conforming control removes only the second IOSTAT= specifier.

Conforming control: `flush_specifiers`.
