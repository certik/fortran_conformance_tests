### S18.10.4-001: Asynchronous communication is performed by non-Fortran procedures with initiation and completion actions

**Source:** S18.10.4 p1.communication-through-nonfortran, p1.initiation-and-completion, p1.pending-communication-affector; J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF547. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Asynchronous communication for an ASYNCHRONOUS Fortran variable occurs through
procedures defined by other means, starts by an initiation procedure, completes by a
completion procedure, and creates pending communication affectors for variables
associated with the asynchronous communication variable between those calls.

### S18.10.4-003: Pending communication affectors obey asynchronous input and output restrictions

**Source:** S18.10.4 p2.input-communication-affector-restrictions, p2.output-communication-affector-restrictions, p2.same-as-async-data-transfer; J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF547. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** For input communication, a pending communication affector shall not be referenced,
defined, undefined, associated with a VALUE dummy, or have pointer association status
