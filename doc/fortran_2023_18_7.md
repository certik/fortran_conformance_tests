### S18.7-001: Allocatable objects in C functions are allocated or deallocated only by CFI allocation functions

**Source:** S18.7 p1.allocatable-only-cfi-allocation; J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF543. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** Within a C function, an allocatable object shall be allocated or deallocated only by
executing CFI_allocate and CFI_deallocate.
