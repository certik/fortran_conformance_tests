### S11.1.2.1-001: Control cannot transfer into a block from outside except for a return from an invoked procedure

**Source:** 11.1.2.1 p1 first sentence; J3/24-007 PDF p203. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** Transfer of control from outside a block to a statement in the block is prohibited. The
exception is the return from a procedure that was invoked within the block. Branching
constructs, labels, and procedure return semantics are separate dependencies.

### S11.1.2.1-002: Transfers within a block and transfers from a block to outside are permitted forms

**Source:** 11.1.2.1 p1 final sentence; J3/24-007 PDF p203. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The language permits transfers within a block and transfers from the interior of a block
to outside the block, subject to the branch, RETURN, EXIT, CYCLE, and construct-specific
