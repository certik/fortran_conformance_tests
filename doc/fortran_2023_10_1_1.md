### S10.1.1-003: A non-variable expression has corank zero

**Source:** 10.1.1 p1, third sentence; J3/24-007 PDF p168. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The corank of an expression that is not a variable is zero. This does not redefine which
expressions are variables, nor the separate rules for coarray variables and coindexed
