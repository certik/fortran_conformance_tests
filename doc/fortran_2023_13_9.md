### S13.9-002: Character string descriptors output their enclosed characters, including blanks and doubled delimiters

**Source:** S13.9, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF307. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A character string edit descriptor writes the enclosed characters, including blanks; its
field width is the number of characters between delimiters, with two consecutive
delimiters counted as one character.
