### S11.1.3.1-001: ASSOCIATE associates named construct entities with expressions or variables during block execution

**Source:** 11.1.3.1 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p204. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The ASSOCIATE construct associates named entities with expressions or variables during
execution of its block. The named construct entities are associating entities and their
names are associate names. Detailed selector evaluation, type, attribute, definability,
and scope effects are owned by 11.1.3.2 and 11.1.3.3 plus Clause 19 dependencies.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `associate-name-source-control`, `construct-entity-scope-source`, `expression-and-variable-selector-classification`.

**Oracle:** This paragraph defines the construct purpose and terminology. It does not by itself
create diagnostics for bad selector syntax or name mismatch; those are R/C units below.

**Oracle limitation:** This source-only catalogue records pending plans only. It creates no Fortran test
program, invokes no processor, approves no fixture or oracle, and claims no coverage.
Future ASSOCIATE fixtures must use non-vacuous selectors and sentinels: lower bounds
must differ where bounds are asserted, character length must be checked with LEN rather
than inferred from padded comparison, outer homonyms must start with values different
from any inner assignment, and assignment through an associate name is credited only
after the selector is established definable. No plan may count selector evaluations,
depend on function side effects, assume a real or complex kind exists, or use compiler
agreement as an oracle.

**Dependencies:** 11.1.3.2 and 11.1.3.3 in this packet;19.4 construct entities NOT YET REGISTERED;19.5.1.6
association NOT YET REGISTERED.
