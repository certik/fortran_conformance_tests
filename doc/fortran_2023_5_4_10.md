### S5.4.10-001: Unallocated allocatable variables are not referenced or defined

**Source:** 5.4.10 p2, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF64. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** An allocatable variable with unallocated allocation status is outside the conforming
source boundary for reference or definition. This restriction is about allocation
status, not allocation syntax or the value that a later allocation stores.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `allocated-reference-control`, `allocated-definition-control`, `unallocated-source-boundary`.

**Oracle:** Positive controls establish allocated status before every reference or definition and
use nondefault sentinels and exact integer values.

**Oracle limitation:** Executing a reference or definition of an unallocated allocatable variable is
nonconforming, and this unnumbered shall does not require a processor diagnostic.
Runtime traps, stale storage contents and diagnostic wording are not portable evidence.

**Dependencies:** 9.7.1.3 and 9.7.3.2 define allocation and deallocation effects; 16.9 ALLOCATED can
observe defined allocation status; coarray cobounds and team facets remain with 5.4.7,
5.4.8 and 8.5.6.
