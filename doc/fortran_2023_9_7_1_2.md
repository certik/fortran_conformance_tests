### S9.7.1.2-001: Allocate-shape specs determine array bounds and zero extents

**Source:** 9.7.1.2 p1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF162. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** When an ALLOCATE statement allocates an array using an allocate-shape-spec-list, the
lower-bound and upper-bound expression values at execution determine the allocated array
bounds. Later redefinition or undefinition of entities used in those expressions does
not alter the bounds. An omitted lower bound is one, and an upper bound less than the
lower bound gives that dimension extent zero and makes the array zero sized.
