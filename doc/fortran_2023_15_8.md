**Source:** 15.8 p1; J3/24-007, 18 December 2023 PDF p361. **Class:** Effect.

specified simple, a procedure defined by a simple subprogram, a dummy procedure or
procedure pointer specified simple, a type-bound procedure bound to a simple procedure,
