### C851: An OPTIONAL entity must be a dummy argument

**Source:** 8.5.12 C851, physical PDF130, printed116 **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** An entity with OPTIONAL must be a dummy argument. Eligible dummy data objects and dummy
procedures retain their other attribute/interface rules. Unlike INTENT's procedure
restriction, this does not require every optional dummy procedure to be a procedure
pointer.

**Oracle limitation:** Required reporting capability is not fatal exit, standardized wording or a printed code.

attribute/interface failures cannot qualify.
