### S13.2.2-001: A character expression format shall have a valid format specification as its leading part

**Source:** S13.2.2, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF289. **Class:** Restriction.

### S13.2.2-002: Positions through the final right parenthesis are defined and stable; trailing positions are irrelevant

**Source:** S13.2.2, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF289. **Class:** Restriction.
