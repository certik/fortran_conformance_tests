### S10.1.8-001: Parenthesized expressions are treated as data entities and preserve parentheses integrity

**Source:** 10.1.8 p1 and NOTE; J3/24-007 PDF p183. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Although other evaluation rules can permit a processor to evaluate a different
expression from the one produced by the expression grammar and interpretation rules,
every expression enclosed in parentheses is treated as a data entity. The processor may
not use a different expression in a way that violates the integrity of those
parentheses.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `parenthesized-expression-data-entity`, `no-rewrite-through-parentheses`, `defined-operation-parentheses-boundary`, `numeric-reassociation-example-caution`, `no-mathematically-equivalent-rewrite-oracle`.

**Oracle:** Future controls should use standard-defined, value-observable distinctions such as
non-associative defined operations to test parenthesized grouping. They must not rely on
floating-point reassociation artifacts, emitted code, operand evaluation order, or
undefined values.

**Oracle limitation:** This source-only catalogue records pending plans only. It creates no Fortran test
program, invokes no processor, approves no fixture or oracle, and claims no coverage.
Future cases must not infer which operand was evaluated, the order of evaluation, or
whether an optional evaluation was omitted; must not assert a result that a permitted
mathematically or logically equivalent rewrite could change; must not use floating-point
tolerance as an escape hatch; must not rely on compiler agreement, address arithmetic,
TRANSFER, emitted assembly, or undefined reads; and must attribute every negative to the
cited requirement rather than to an independently invalid rule owned elsewhere.
