### S8.5.8.6-002: Initializer extents and stated or default lower bounds determine implied-shape bounds

**Source:** 8.5.8.6 p3, physical PDF127, lines32-34 **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Each dimension takes its extent from the corresponding dimension of the constant
