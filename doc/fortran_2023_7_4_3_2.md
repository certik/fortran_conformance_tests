## Source and oracle boundaries

Double precision must have decimal precision at least ten, decimal
exponent range at least thirty-seven, and greater decimal precision than
default real. The default-real precision-six and range-thirty-seven
sentences are recommendations, not mandatory thresholds.

Approximation methods and numeric models do not imply IEEE encoding,
storage size, correct rounding or complete actual value sets. Kind
identifiers are not widths; selected kinds can coincide. Default and
double are different because their decimal precisions differ. Selector
ties minimize precision and then kind number under SELECTED_REAL_KIND,
not range. No arbitrary identifier is narrowed to another kind.

The authored zero effects compare mathematical values. They retain the
sign-distinguishing-processor condition and explicit intrinsic exceptions,
including SIGN. They neither require signed-zero support nor compare
zero bit patterns. Decimal conversion effects remain pending until an
independent, versioned conversion contract supplies their exact oracle;
compiler consensus and arbitrary tolerances cannot provide that premise.

The complete sixteen-method inventory, six-code numeric-suffix family,
bounded exact model arithmetic, long significands and rational conversion
family remain pending for a following bounded pass. This pass does not
claim their completion through default/double inquiries or the legacy
4/8 control. Actual value sets, independent zero-sign distinction and the
remaining intrinsic zero-use matrix also remain evidence gaps.

## Diagnostics and legacy integration

Each authored negative has a minimal conforming control and an exact
literal source point or declared token relation. Qualified message routes
must identify the actual grammar or unsupported-value cause. Generic
type/kind/constant/exponent words, already-constant-expression complaints,
unsupported facilities, crashes, verifier and resource failures cannot
substitute. C722's unsupported REAL kind is itself a reporting obligation,
so it must not be discarded by a blanket unsupported-message exclusion.
Numbered reporting does not require a fatal exit. No prose rejection
policy is introduced; future non-required diagnostic cases would need an
explicit, separately reviewed policy.

Both retained C722 Fortran bodies and all six execution IDs are preserved.
Only metadata headers are added. Adjacent `C722_invalid.cases.json`
assigns each of the five isolated negatives its own facet, review key and
causal contract, using its actual marker line. It deliberately composes
the existing kind-seven-absence probe with a new receiving/default-model
qualification. Five minimally repaired compile controls retain the
original contexts, including both repeated array-constructor suffixes.

The retained runtime positive is explicitly processor-profile evidence.
Its narrow independent profile requires REAL32=4, REAL64=8 and double kind
equal to REAL64, making its assignments same-kind. It retains the exact

representation or IEEE layout. Its repeated same-kind literal comparisons
are not independent rational-value oracles. New unprofiled named-selector
controls and mandatory default/double effects remain separate.

The source-plan review is not approval of these unwritten-at-review-time

qualifying current reference cause and remains a source-only/needs-oracle

pending; the existing pair is neither copied nor newly linked.

Retained-container activation requires the coordinator's harness overlay.
The local index registration and all overlay files are excluded from the
author's implementation commit; the observational ledger is a separate

### R713: A signed real literal has an optional leading sign

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 R713, PDF 82, printed 68, line 10. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** A signed-real-literal-constant consists of an R714 literal with either no leading sign
or one R712 sign. Plus and minus are the existing sign alternatives. The sign on an
exponent is a different occurrence of the shared syntax. An arbitrary expression with
unary operators is not necessarily a signed real literal.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `unsigned`, `plus`, `minus`, `signed-suffixed`, `single-leading-sign`.

**Oracle:** Compile-only DATA controls use matching kinds and zero payloads for omitted/plus/minus
signs and both signed named-suffix forms. The double-leading-minus negative has a
one-character repair and a narrow unexpected-minus token contract. No generic
DATA/expected-token fallback is counted.

**Oracle limitation:** R712 retains sign ownership; R710 retains exponent-sign ownership. The initial snapshot
supplies no qualifying reference route for the extra-minus case: the target accepts it,
GNU reports generic DATA syntax, and Flang's parenthesis recovery is not the declared
cause. Its source/contract therefore remains an explicit source-only/needs-oracle review
candidate, not approved diagnostic coverage; the unexpected-minus route is prospective,
not a claimed observed message. Source echoes, unrelated recovery, unsupported
facilities, crashes, verifier and resource failures cannot satisfy the contract.
Required reporting does not prescribe a fatal exit.

**Dependencies:** R714/R712; 8.6.7/R848 and p8/p10, PDF 137-138; 4.1.2 p2 and 4.2 p2(3), PDF 45-46. Real
signed-zero semantics remain S7.4.3.2-003.

### R714: Real literals have significand and exponent-bearing alternatives

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 R714, PDF 82, printed 68, lines 11-12. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** A real literal either has a significand with an optional exponent-letter/exponent pair,
or has a digit string followed by a required exponent-letter/exponent pair. Either
alternative may end with an underscore and kind-param. An underscore without its kind
parameter is incomplete. A sign before the entire literal belongs to R713 or to an
enclosing expression.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `significand-only`, `significand-exponent`, `digits-exponent`, `significand-suffix`, `significand-exponent-suffix`, `digits-exponent-suffix`, `suffix-needs-kind`.

**Oracle:** Six finite zero-valued compile controls distinguish the two top-level alternatives,
exponent omission/presence and suffix omission/presence. One empty-suffix negative has a
one-token conforming repair and a diagnostic located at that literal.

**Oracle limitation:** R709 owns kind-param alternatives, R711 owns digit strings and R715-R717 own
descendants. In particular, a bare digit string can be a valid integer constant and is
not an isolated R714 negative in DATA or assignment. Parenthesized suffix expressions
remain a canonical R709 issue rather than a new local rule. No literal magnitude or
decimal-rounding conclusion is credited to these admission controls.

**Dependencies:** R709/R711, R715-R717, C721/C722; 6.1.4/R602 and 8.6.7/R848, PDF 67 and 137. 4.2 p2(3),
PDF 46, supplies detection/reporting rather than mandatory rejection.

### R715: A significand contains a decimal point and at least one digit

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 R715, PDF 82, printed 68, lines 13-14. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The significand alternatives allow digits on both sides of a decimal point, digits
before a trailing point, or digits after a leading point. At least one digit is present.
The dot is not optional within a significand, even though R714 separately permits a
digit-string/exponent real literal.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `digits-both-sides`, `trailing-point`, `leading-point`, `digit-required`.

**Oracle:** The three positive forms are compile-only controls with matching object kinds and zero
payloads. The negative removes the only digit from the leading-point form, keeping the
actual DATA constant context and one-to-one initialization intact.

**Oracle limitation:** R711 remains the digit-string owner. A missing point in a valid exponent-bearing real or
an ordinary integer constant is not a negative. Value accuracy, leading-zero semantics
and exponent scaling require their separate effect oracles. The reporting contract is a
cause-specific located grammar route, not the substring real or constant.

**Dependencies:** R714/R711 and 6.1.3; 8.6.7/R848 and p8/p10, PDF 137-138. The finite source uses the
established free-form character interface.

### R716: The standard real exponent letters are E and D

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 R716, PDF 82, printed 68, lines 15-16. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** E and D are the alternatives for exponent-letter. Their case-insensitive source
spellings follow the existing letter-case rule outside character contexts. The literal's
kind and the restriction on combining an exponent letter with a suffix are separate
semantic and constraint requirements.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `e-letter`, `d-letter`, `nonstandard-letter`.

**Oracle:** Finite E/e and D/d compile controls establish admission in the actual exponent position.
The Q-to-D pair admits the observed target Q0-token report and GNU's explicit Q-exponent
extension report. Flang's exact located Q-exponent portability message is allowed
nonfatally; no compiler warning flag or mandatory rejection is invented.

**Oracle limitation:** No portable Q kind, quad representation or storage width is asserted. Generic exponent
nouns or unsupported precision facilities cannot credit the negative. R716 does not
itself establish that E is default real or D is double precision; those effects are
S7.4.3.2-006.

**Dependencies:** 6.1.2 p3 and S6.1.2-002, PDF 67; R714/R717/C721; 4.2 p2(3) and p4, PDF 46, permit
extensions only with the required reporting capability.

### C721: An exponent-bearing literal with a kind suffix uses E

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 C721 (R714), PDF 82, printed 68, line 18. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** When both an exponent-letter and kind-param occur in a real literal, the letter must be
E. A D exponent with a suffix is not permitted even when the suffix denotes the
double-precision kind. A suffix without an exponent letter and a D exponent without a
suffix do not violate this constraint.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `suffix-without-exponent`, `e-with-suffix`, `d-without-suffix`, `d-with-named-suffix`, `d-with-digit-suffix`.

**Oracle:** The no-exponent suffix, E-plus-suffix and unsuffixed-D forms are compile controls. The
authored named-suffix pair changes only D to E with a supported double-kind selector and
zero payload. GNU's joint D/exponent/explicit-kind cause and Flang's exact located
non-E-plus-kind portability report qualify; the latter need not reject. The optional
digit-suffix matrix remains pending.

**Oracle limitation:** A generic invalid kind, unavailable method, exponent, constant-expression or
parser-summary message is not sufficient. In particular, a constancy complaint about
kd=KIND(0.0D0) is a wrong cause. Numeric branch profiles establish real-kind support
without compiling the invalid D-suffix form. The proposed message routes are semantic
predicates awaiting exact phase-2 diagnostic qualification, not invented standard
wording.

**Dependencies:** R714/R716/R709, C718/C719/C722; 16.9.118 and 16.10.2.26, PDF 424 and 476; 10.1.12 p1,
PDF 187-188; 4.2 p2(3), PDF 46.

### S7.4.3.2-001: At least two real approximation methods have representation and kind identities

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 p1, PDF 81, printed 67, lines 12-15. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The processor supplies at least two approximation methods defining real value sets. Each
has a representation method and is identified by a KIND parameter value, returned by
KIND for an entity using that method. The sets approximate mathematical reals; they are
not the unbounded real line, an IEEE format specification or a physical storage-layout
contract.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `two-method-minimum`, `default-double-membership`, `finite-method-inventory`, `actual-value-set-evidence`.

**Oracle:** Runtime type/kind and inventory relationships provide a finite necessary witness.
Default and double kinds are distinct because p5 requires different decimal precisions.
Additional inquiry-selected kinds may coincide with either or with each other. Actual
representation/value-set evidence remains a separate pending source/profile contract.

**Oracle limitation:** The sixteen-slot authoring boundary does not weaken the unprofiled two-method
requirement. No kind is narrowed into another integer kind or converted to real to
compare identifiers. Decimal text may be parsed as arbitrary-precision host integers in
future external oracles. KIND order, storage size and numeric model shape are not
interchangeable.

**Dependencies:** 7.4.1 p2/S7.4.1-001, PDF 80; 16.4 p1/p3/p4, PDF 366-367; 16.9.118, PDF 424; 16.10.2.26,
PDF 476. The p1 domain sentence is separately classified as a definition.

### S7.4.3.2-002: Real methods expose decimal precision, exponent range, radix and criterion-based selection

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 p2, PDF 81, printed 67, lines 16-19; canonical intrinsic definitions supply the inquiry and selection contracts. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** PRECISION, RANGE and RADIX return the corresponding properties of a real approximation
method. SELECTED_REAL_KIND selects using requested decimal precision, exponent range and
radix. Under its canonical definition, present precision/range are lower bounds, omitted
precision/range act as zero, and omitted radix places no radix condition. Among
qualifying kinds it minimizes decimal precision, then the numeric kind value if that
precision ties; range does not supply a further tie-breaker.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `precision-inquiry`, `range-inquiry`, `radix-inquiry`, `default-integer-inquiry-results`, `precision-selection`, `range-selection`, `radix-selection`, `selection-ties`.

**Oracle:** Mandatory selection controls run independently of bounded model or inventory profiles.
In the complete bounded inventory, external exact arithmetic determines the expected
selector from all qualifying methods; it must not merely reselect an object's kind and
demand identity. Default-integer results and actual selected-object properties are
separately checked.

**Oracle limitation:** This is a local connection to canonical intrinsic behavior, not full ownership or
coverage of all SELECTED_REAL_KIND argument and failure-code rules. Unexpected negative
results for the guaranteed requests fail. Optional requests and failure codes must never
be used as supposedly supported selectors. Equal precision or range does not imply equal
kind, representation or radix. Physical format, correct rounding and total value sets
remain outside these inquiry witnesses.

**Dependencies:** 16.4, PDF 366-367; 16.9.71/96/118/136/142/162/166/170/183/209, PDF 403, 416, 424, 431,
434-435, 445-447, 449, 455-456 and 466. 10.1.12 permits the inquiry-based constant
selectors. C.3.1, PDF 583-584, is informative guidance, not a promise about kind
numbers.

### S7.4.3.2-003: Real zero exists and sign-distinguishing zeros are mathematically equivalent with explicit exceptions

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 p3, PDF 81, printed 67, lines 20-24; informative NOTE 2 on PDF 82. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Real includes zero. If a processor distinguishes positive and negative real zero, it
treats them as mathematically equivalent in every intrinsic relational operation and as
intrinsic-procedure actual arguments, except where a procedure explicitly distinguishes
negative zero. This does not require a processor to distinguish zeros or require their
stored representations to be identical.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `zero-exists`, `all-relational-spellings`, `mixed-kind-and-type-relations`, `sign-distinguishing-profile`, `finite-intrinsic-arguments`, `explicit-sign-exception`, `remaining-intrinsic-use-matrix`.

**Oracle:** The relational truth matrix is exact mathematical equality, not a bit comparison. The
four initially selected intrinsic routes have defined zero inputs and source-defined
mathematical expectations. SIGN's explicit exception is retained rather than incorrectly
applying the general equivalence rule to it.

**Oracle limitation:** No division by zero, signed-zero bit pattern, IEEE support, negative-zero preservation
in every operation, or identical MIN/MAX zero representations is assumed. A missing
optional distinction profile limits that additional witness only; unprofiled
zero/equality cases remain runtime effects. There is no invented mandatory diagnostic
for these prose effects.

**Dependencies:** 10.1.5.5, PDF 180; 16.9.2 ABS (PDF 376), 16.9.110 INT (PDF 421-422), 16.9.135 MAX (PDF
431), 16.9.141 MIN (PDF 434), 16.9.189 SIGN including case(iv) across PDF 457-458.
Clause 17 exception routes require their own source review before use.

### S7.4.3.2-004: Default and double-precision declarations select real kinds with strictly ordered decimal precision

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 p5, PDF 82, printed 68, lines 3-6. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** REAL without a kind parameter denotes the default real method, whose kind is KIND(0.0).
DOUBLE PRECISION denotes a real method whose kind is KIND(0.0D0). The latter method has
strictly greater decimal precision than the default method. No analogous strict
exponent-range ordering or portable kind-code ordering is specified here.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `default-declaration-kind`, `double-declaration-kind`, `double-is-real`, `strict-decimal-precision`.

**Oracle:** Runtime inquiry comparisons and explicit dummy type/kind agreement directly test the
declaration semantics. The precision inequality is unconditional and independently
retained even when a selected-kind inquiry has an implementation failure.

**Oracle limitation:** R704 retains keyword syntax. No default-32/double-64 storage premise, fixed radix,
greater RANGE, or numerical ordering of the two kind identifiers is introduced. The
strictly different precisions imply different default/double kind identities; other
selected methods may alias either one.

**Dependencies:** R704/R706/C717 and 7.4.1 p2, PDF 80; 16.9.118/162, PDF 424 and 445; 15.5.2.5 for
explicit intrinsic dummy agreement. The keyword restatements in p4 map to R704 rather
than new local syntax requirements.

### S7.4.3.2-006: Real literal kind follows suffix presence and E or D exponent spelling

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 p7, PDF 82, printed 68, lines 20-22. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** An unsuffixed real literal is default real when its exponent part is absent or uses E.

literal has that specified supported real kind. Assignment to a differently declared
object does not retroactively choose the literal's kind.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Oracle:** KIND is applied to the literal before any receiving-object conversion. Explicit matching
real dummies provide a separate type/kind admission check. Zero payloads avoid a
decimal-accuracy dependency for these kind effects.

**Oracle limitation:** The declaration keyword and suffix grammar keep their canonical owners. The digit-code
set is a finite profile family, not a universal list of real kinds; unavailability
cannot erase the unprofiled named/default/double effects. Arbitrary kind values stay
default integers and are never narrowed or interpreted as bytes.

**Dependencies:** R714-R717/C721/C722; R709/C718/C719; 16.9.118, PDF 424; 10.2.1.3 p8/Table 10.9, PDF 191,

### S7.4.3.2-007: A real exponent gives decimal power-of-ten scaling

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 p8, PDF 82, printed 68, lines 23-24. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The exponent specifies decimal power-of-ten scaling of the significand or digit string.
Real literal notation therefore denotes decimal scientific values even on a
nondecimal-radix processor. This mathematical denotation does not by itself prescribe
the processor's conversion accuracy, rounding direction or stored representation.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `zero-exponent`, `positive-exponent`, `negative-exponent`, `digit-string-scaling`, `equivalent-decimal-forms`, `nonexact-decimal-conversion`.

**Oracle:** The source denotation is calculated exactly as a rational numerator times a power of
ten. The executable oracle must separately justify how that rational maps to the
selected processor method, including any arithmetic used to build the expected value.
Initial profile candidates are documented radix-two p=24/53/113 methods and a documented
decimal method if available, not assumed IEEE formats.

**Oracle limitation:** An exactly representable model value does not independently establish a universal
literal-conversion accuracy promise. RANGE/PRECISION/RADIX alone cannot ratify physical
format or correct rounding. Overflow, underflow, an out-of-domain value, or unknown
approximation must not be assigned a fixed standard oracle. No out-of-range prose
negative or permissive compiler option is planned.

**Dependencies:** 7.4.3.2 p1/p6/p9 and 16.4, PDF 81-82 and 366-367; 16.9.172 REAL, PDF 449-450, explicitly
uses processor-dependent approximation. 10.2.1.3 p8, PDF 191, supplies subsequent
numeric assignment conversion, not literal rounding.

### S7.4.3.2-008: A significand may contain more digits than the method uses in its approximation

**Source:** 7.4.3.2 p9, PDF 82, printed 68, line 25. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** A real literal may be written with excess significand digits beyond those the processor
uses for the approximation. This permission is not a requirement to retain every digit
or to round the value in a particular way.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `long-zero-tail`, `long-nonzero-tail`.

**Oracle:** Successful compilation of two finite, bounded-magnitude long-significand controls
demonstrates permitted source forms for the qualified methods. Their source length,
literal kind and range must already be valid.

**Oracle limitation:** No maximum processor precision or arbitrary source-size limit is inferred. If 64 digits
do not exceed the qualified method's precision, these controls do not demonstrate the
excess-digits facet; extend the finite reviewed family rather than mislabeling it. There
is no invalid twin and no invented mandatory rejection for this prose permission.

**Dependencies:** R714/R715; 6.3.2.1 source limits and the canonical continuation rules; 16.4 and

decimal conversion error bound.
