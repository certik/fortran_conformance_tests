### S11.1.5.2-001: CHANGE TEAM execution uses corresponding FORM TEAM values, changes the current team for the block, and restores it at END TEAM

**Source:** 11.1.5.2 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p207. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The team-values on active images executing the CHANGE TEAM statement shall be team
variables defined by corresponding executions of the same FORM TEAM statement, and the
current team at execution shall be the team current when those variables were defined.
Statements in the CHANGE TEAM block execute with the current team identified by the
team-value. A variable team-value shall not be defined or become undefined during the
construct. Completing the construct by END TEAM restores the original current team.

**Diagnostic obligation:** context-dependent.

### S11.1.5.2-005: Successful CHANGE TEAM execution synchronizes all images of the new team at the same statement count

**Source:** 11.1.5.2 p5; J3/24-007 PDF p208. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Successful execution of CHANGE TEAM performs implicit synchronization of all images of
the new team. As a dynamic multi-image obligation on the program, all active images of
the new team execute the same CHANGE TEAM statement, and each image's segment following
the statement is delayed until all other images of that team have executed the same
statement the same number of times in the original team. A conforming processor is not
required to diagnose violations of the same-statement or same-count obligation.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `successful-change-team-sync-source-control`, `all-active-images-same-statement-source-control`, `same-statement-count-violation-unimplementable-source-control`, `post-change-segment-delay-source-control`.
