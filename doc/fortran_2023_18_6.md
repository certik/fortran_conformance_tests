### S18.6-001: C descriptors are initialized, updated, or copied only by specified CFI functions

**Source:** S18.6 p1; J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF543. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** A C descriptor shall not be initialized, updated, or copied except by calling the
functions specified in 18.5.5.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `c-descriptor-managed-only-by-cfi-functions`.
