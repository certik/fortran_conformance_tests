### S17.11.1-001: Detailed procedure descriptions use generic names and default procedure conventions

**Source:** S17.11.1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF487. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** In 17.11 detailed descriptions, procedure names are generic rather than specific; all
functions are simple and all subroutines impure unless otherwise stated; dummy arguments
