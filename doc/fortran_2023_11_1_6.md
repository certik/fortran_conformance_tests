### S11.1.6-003: CRITICAL mutual exclusion and segment ordering are ensured across images

**Source:** 11.1.6 p3; J3/24-007 PDF p209. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The processor shall ensure that once an image has commenced executing the CRITICAL
block, no other image commences executing it until that image completes execution of the
construct. The image shall not execute an image control statement during the block. The
sequence of executed statements is a segment. If one image completes without failing and
another is next, the first image's segment precedes the second; if the first completes
by failing, its previous segment precedes the next image's segment.

**Diagnostic obligation:** context-dependent.

**Facets:** `mutual-exclusion-source-control`, `no-image-control-during-block-source-control`, `critical-block-sequence-is-segment`, `nonfailed-critical-segment-precedence`, `failed-critical-segment-precedence-source-control`.

**Oracle:** This requirement owns mutual exclusion and the local segment-order relationships, not
fairness, which image is next, lock implementation, or arbitrary ordering outside the
stated segments. Mutual exclusion and cross-image precedence are recorded as
source-control/documentary evidence only in this packet: single-image runs are vacuous
and timing, contention, races, hangs, or scheduler behavior are not portable
