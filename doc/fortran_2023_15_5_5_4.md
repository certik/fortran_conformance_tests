### S15.5.5.4-001: Unestablished dummy-argument names that are referenced as procedures are dummy procedures

**Source:** S15.5.5.4, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF352. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** For an unestablished name that is the name of a dummy argument, the dummy argument is a
dummy procedure and the reference invokes the effective argument corresponding to that
dummy procedure.
