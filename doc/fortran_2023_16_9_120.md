### S16.9.120-001: LCOBOUND COARRAY allocation/designator, DIM, and KIND restrictions

**Source:** S16.9.120, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF425. **Class:** Restriction.

designator shall have nonzero corank in the rightmost part-ref; DIM shall be an integer
scalar in range one through the corank and its actual argument shall not be an optional
