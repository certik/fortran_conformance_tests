### S10.1.6.2-001: A defined operation is interpreted by the function that defines it

**Source:** 10.1.6.2 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p182. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** After a defined operation has been established under 10.1.6.1, its interpretation is the
value and characteristics provided by the defining function, with operand-to-dummy
correspondence supplied by the defined-operation interface rules.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `unary-operation-function-interpretation`, `binary-operation-function-interpretation`, `direct-call-control`, `no-intrinsic-fallback-for-applicable-defined-operation`.

**Oracle:** Future positives derive the expected result from the defining function semantics and
literal operand components. They must not infer a call count, call order, short-circuit
decision, or emitted implementation strategy.

**Oracle limitation:** This source-only catalogue records pending plans only. It creates no Fortran test
program, invokes no processor, approves no fixture or oracle, and claims no coverage.
Future cases must not infer which operand was evaluated, the order of evaluation, or
whether an optional evaluation was omitted; must not assert a result that a permitted
mathematically or logically equivalent rewrite could change; must not use floating-point
tolerance as an escape hatch; must not rely on compiler agreement, address arithmetic,
TRANSFER, emitted assembly, or undefined reads; and must attribute every negative to the
cited requirement rather than to an independently invalid rule owned elsewhere.

**Dependencies:** 10.1.6.1;10.1.6.3;10.1.7;10.1.8;15.4.3.4.2;15.5.

### S10.1.6.2-002: Symbolic and named relational operator spellings have the same interpretations

**Source:** 10.1.6.2 p2; J3/24-007 PDF p182. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The relational operator pairs < and .LT., <= and .LE., > and .GT., >= and .GE., == and
.EQ., and /= and .NE. always have the same interpretations respectively, including when
a relational intrinsic-operator spelling is extended by a defined operation under the
interface rules.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `symbolic-less-same-as-lt`, `symbolic-less-equal-same-as-le`, `symbolic-greater-same-as-gt`, `symbolic-greater-equal-same-as-ge`, `symbolic-equal-same-as-eq`, `symbolic-not-equal-same-as-ne`, `relational-defined-extension-paired-spellings`.

**Oracle:** Future controls compare paired spellings in matched contexts. Character relational
controls must account for blank padding of the shorter operand; this requirement owns
spelling equivalence, not character collation or relational operand validity.

**Oracle limitation:** This source-only catalogue records pending plans only. It creates no Fortran test
program, invokes no processor, approves no fixture or oracle, and claims no coverage.
Future cases must not infer which operand was evaluated, the order of evaluation, or
whether an optional evaluation was omitted; must not assert a result that a permitted
mathematically or logically equivalent rewrite could change; must not use floating-point
tolerance as an escape hatch; must not rely on compiler agreement, address arithmetic,
TRANSFER, emitted assembly, or undefined reads; and must attribute every negative to the
