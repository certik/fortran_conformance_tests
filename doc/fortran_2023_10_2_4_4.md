### S10.2.4.4-001: Many-to-one assignment is multiple assignments to one object or multiple pointer targets for one pointer

**Source:** 10.2.4.4 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p201. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A many-to-one assignment is more than one assignment to the same object, or association
of more than one target with the same pointer, whether the object is referenced directly
or indirectly through a pointer.

### S10.2.4.4-003: The same object may be assigned or pointer-assigned in different FORALL body statements

**Source:** 10.2.4.4 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p201. **Class:** Effect.
