### S15.4.3.4.3-001: ASSIGNMENT generic specifications require subroutines referenced as defined assignments

### S15.4.3.4.3-002: Defined assignment subroutines have two suitable dummy arguments and differ from intrinsic assignment

**Source:** 15.4.3.4.3, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF330. **Class:** Restriction.

### S15.4.3.4.3-003: Defined assignment is a subroutine reference with left and right sides as actual arguments

**Source:** 15.4.3.4.3, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF330. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A defined assignment is treated as a subroutine reference: the left-hand side is the
first argument, the right-hand side enclosed in parentheses is the second argument,
applicable actual-argument restrictions apply, and the ASSIGNMENT generic specification
extends or redefines assignment.
