### S10.1.2.7-001: Level-4 expressions are the relational-operator layer over level-3 expressions

**Source:** 10.1.2.7 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p170. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** Level-4 expressions are level-3 expressions optionally involving one relational
operator. The grammar admits a level-3 expression by itself or one relation between two
level-3 expressions; relation interpretation is separate.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `level-3-base`, `single-relation-layer`, `no-unparenthesized-relation-chain`.

**Oracle:** Plans must distinguish grammar admission from relational truth rules and avoid chained

### R1013: A level-4 expression is either level-3 or one relational operation between level-3 expressions

**Source:** 10.1.2.7 R1013; J3/24-007 PDF p170. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The level-4-expr syntax is a level-3-expr, optionally preceded by another level-3-expr
and rel-op. Thus an unparenthesized level-4 expression contains at most one relational
operator at this grammar level.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

### R1014: Relational operators have twelve spellings

**Source:** 10.1.2.7 R1014; J3/24-007 PDF pp170-171. **Class:** Syntax.
