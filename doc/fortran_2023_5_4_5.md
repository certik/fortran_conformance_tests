### S5.4.5-001: Passing a procedure designator as an actual argument does not execute it unless execution is needed

**Source:** 5.4.5 p2, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF63. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The appearance of a procedure designator as an actual argument does not constitute a
reference to that procedure unless such a reference is necessary to complete the
specification of the actual argument.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.
