### S11.1.7.3-001: A DO construct is initially inactive and becomes active only when its DO statement is executed

**Source:** 11.1.7.3 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p212. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A DO construct is either active or inactive. It is initially inactive and becomes active
only when its DO statement is executed.
