### S12.2.1-001: Records are value or character sequences with three kinds

**Source:** S12.2.1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF242. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A record is a sequence of values or a sequence of characters, need not correspond to a
physical entity, and has one of three kinds: formatted, unformatted, or endfile.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.
