### S9.7.1.3-001: Allocated status of allocatable variables and its consequences

**Source:** 9.7.1.3 p1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF163. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** An allocatable entity has allocation status. The status of an allocatable variable
becomes allocated when it is allocated by ALLOCATE, allocated during assignment, or
given that status by MOVE_ALLOC. An allocated allocatable variable may be referenced,
defined, or deallocated; allocating it causes an ALLOCATE error condition; and ALLOCATED
returns true for it.

**Facets:** `allocated-by-allocate`, `allocated-by-assignment`, `allocated-by-move-alloc`, `allocated-reference-define-deallocate`, `allocating-allocated-variable-error`, `allocated-inquiry-true`.

### S9.7.1.3-002: Unallocated status of allocatable variables and its consequences

**Source:** 9.7.1.3 p1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF163. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** An allocatable variable is unallocated if it is not allocated. It becomes unallocated
when deallocated or given that status by MOVE_ALLOC. An unallocated allocatable variable
shall not be referenced or defined and shall not be supplied as an actual argument to a
nonallocatable nonoptional dummy argument except to certain intrinsic inquiry functions.
It may be allocated by ALLOCATE. Deallocating it causes a DEALLOCATE error condition,
and ALLOCATED returns false for it.
