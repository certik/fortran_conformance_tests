### S6.2.1-001: The lexical-token union admits its listed categories and spellings

**Source:** 6.2.1 p1, second sentence; J3/24-007 PDF page 68, printed page 54, lines 8-10. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The program-unit lexical classification admits statement keywords, names, noncomplex
literal constants, operators, statement labels, the delimiter category, and the
explicitly listed .NIL., comma, =, =>, :, ::, ;, .., ?, and % spellings. A complex
literal constant is excluded as a single lexical token, not prohibited as a compound
literal. Each alternative still needs a legal enclosing grammar and source form. A named
constant uses a name token, not an additional literal category. The = assignment symbol
is a separate alternative: it is neither a defined operator nor a member of the
delimiter list in 6.2.6.

**Diagnostic obligation:** context-dependent.

**Facets:** `keyword`, `name`, `integer-literal`, `real-literal`, `logical-literal`, `character-literal`, `boz-literal`, `nil`, `operator`, `statement-label`, `delimiter`, `comma`, `equals`, `arrow`, `colon`, `double-colon`, `semicolon`, `double-dot`, `question-mark`, `percent`.

**Oracle:** All fourteen programs are positive controls for admission, with finite self-checks
supplied by their enclosing constructs. The basic control checks two separately declared
and assigned integers. Separate literal controls check a default real, both logical
values, a character value containing punctuation, and the integer DATA value of B'101'
(5). Further controls check parenthesized integer arithmetic, a forward labeled branch,
an in-bounds array section, association with a defined scalar TARGET, two
semicolon-separated assignments, a scalar actual argument's rank, and a scalar
derived-type component. The question-mark control selects 17 and 29 in separate
true/false evaluations. The .NIL. control independently checks optional-argument
presence for true/false selections; it accesses the argument only inside a PRESENT
guard. All objects read have been defined, no loop is needed, and the absent argument is
never evaluated.

**Oracle limitation:** This finite union plan does not establish complete canonical name, literal, operator,
label, delimiter, or construct semantics. The five literal facets refine just the
noncomplex-literal member; no complex-literal rejection is invented. Ordinary valid
programs do not reveal the processor's token count or prove the complex-literal
exclusion, so that definitional boundary remains source-only. The controls neither
exhaust keyword/operator spellings and kinds nor test source-form blank, continuation,
or token-boundary rules. Punctuation inside a character literal is not credited as
independent punctuation or delimiter tokens. The two F2023 conditional controls are
separate from each other and from assumed-rank support; .NIL. nevertheless necessarily
depends on conditional-argument syntax. No negative case is allocated: a real diagnostic
obligation would have to be justified by the applicable numbered grammar/constraint or
Clause 6 source-form/character rule under 4.2 p2(3)/(5), not by treating this
classification as a context-free illegal-character blacklist.

**Dependencies:** 5.5.2 p1 defines the unqualified statement keyword (PDF 65). Canonical ownership stays
with 6.2.2 R603/C601, 6.2.3 R604-R607/C602, 6.2.4 R608-R610 and the Clause 10 operator
rules, 6.2.5 R611/C603 and prose, and 6.2.6. R605 and 7.4.3.1/R708, 7.4.3.2/R714,
7.4.4.3/R724, 7.4.5/R725, and 7.7/R772-R776 supply the five noncomplex literal
categories. R718 and 6.3.2.2 p1 explain the complex-literal boundary (PDF 83 and 71).
C7119, 8.6.7 p8/p11, and 16.9.110 p5 case (vi) justify the small, zero-padded BOZ DATA
value without a sign-bit assumption (PDF 114, 138, 422). R401/R801, R1033, R1001 and
10.1.3, R1159, R922, R1034 with C1016/C1022/C1024/C1028 and 10.2.2.3 p1-p2, 6.3.2.5 p2,
and R911/R913 supply the ordinary punctuation contexts. R827/C839/C840 and 16.9.171 p5

controls are marked F2023 and are not optional-profile skips; a reference
compiler's F2018 fallback cannot establish their F2023 validity by rejecting
them. No compiler outcome approves this draft or changes its oracle.

## Boundaries and handoff

The source-form dependencies were read in 6.3.1-6.3.3, but their blank,
comment, continuation, and statement-boundary obligations are not allocated
here. In particular, the semicolon control is one legal free-form use, not
complete source-form coverage. Names, constants, operators, and labels retain
their existing catalogue ownership; delimiter spellings belong to 6.2.6.
The `.NIL.` case cannot avoid the dependency on conditional-argument support,
but its failure cannot mask any of the other independently compiled files.

Independent source/oracle review is still required, including any source-only
adjudication needed when a mandatory F2023 feature lacks reference support.
No review or expected-failure baseline is changed by this authoring unit.
Scoped source accounting does not close the unfinished whole-source census.
