### S7.5.6.1-001: Finalizability is a recursive type and nonpointer-entity classification

**Source:** 7.5.6.1, p2, p2.type-has-final-subroutine, p2.plain-finalizable-component, p2.nonpointer-data-entity, p2.entity-type-condition, p2.all-other-entities-excluded; J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, PDF 102. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A derived type is finalizable exactly when it has a final subroutine or a nonpointer
nonallocatable component of finalizable type. A nonpointer data entity is finalizable
exactly when its type is finalizable; no other entity is finalizable. A pointer entity
and its target are different, and allocated allocatable subobjects can be finalized by
their own deallocation events without making the containing type finalizable through
this recursion.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `own-final-type-classification`, `plain-component-recursion`, `pointer-recursion-boundary`, `allocatable-recursion-boundary`, `nonpointer-entity-classification`, `pointer-result-and-target-use-graph`.

**Oracle:** Classification requires reviewed source/use relations, not a fabricated runtime inquiry
or a no-op parser success. Concrete users have separately declared events and
independently initialized observable state.

**Oracle limitation:** No mandatory callback follows merely from a finalizable type: selection and event
conditions remain separate. Allocatable-component cleanup and unreachable pointer-target
choices are not collapsed into the recursive classification.

**Dependencies:** 7.5.6.2-.4;3.30, PDF22;7.5.7.2;9.4.2;9.7.3.2-.3, PDF165-166;19.5/19.6.
