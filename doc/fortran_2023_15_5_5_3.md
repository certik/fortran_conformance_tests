### S15.5.5.3-001: EXTERNAL procedure names resolve to pointer targets, dummy effective arguments, or external procedures

**Source:** S15.5.5.3, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF351. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** For a name with the EXTERNAL attribute, a procedure pointer reference is to its target,
a nonpointer dummy procedure reference is to the corresponding effective argument, and
any other reference is to the external procedure with that name.
