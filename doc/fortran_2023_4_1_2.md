# Fortran 2023: 4.1.2 Constraints

Draft catalogue: `doc/catalogues/syntax_constraints.json`. Source: pinned
J3/24-007, dated 18 December 2023, PDF 45, printed 31, paragraphs 1-3.

All three base units and nine fine units have source dispositions. They
define constraint numbering, parenthesized syntax association, propagation
to every occurrence of the term, and the distinction between broader prose
restrictions and a numbered diagnostic subset. No new local S requirement
or executable is justified by those conventions.

The actual processor reporting obligation is not discarded: it is owned
by `S4.2-003`, whose capability-aggregation facets remain pending. Other
4.2 obligations also apply outside the R/C inventory, including Clause 6
source restrictions and Clause 19 scope rules. Conversely, neither a
prose `shall` nor a numbered identifier by itself requires fatal rejection,
a specific invocation mode, or a rule code in a diagnostic. The non-FORMAT
format-specification exception must be applied separately.

Independent source review is still required. The legacy `S15.5.2.4`
namespace refers to 15.5.2.5 p2-p3 and remains unchanged; supported-kind
argument disagreement is not the unsupported-kind obligation in 4.2 p2(4).
