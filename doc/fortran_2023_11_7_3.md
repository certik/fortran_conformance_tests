### S11.7.3-001: Successful SYNC ALL synchronizes all images in the current team and orders surrounding segments

**Source:** 11.7.3 p2; J3/24-007 PDF p232. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** Successful execution of SYNC ALL synchronizes all images in the current team. The
following segment on image M is delayed until every other image in the current team has
executed SYNC ALL as many times as M in the team, and segments before SYNC ALL on one
image precede segments after SYNC ALL on another image.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `sync-all-synchronizes-current-team-source-control`, `sync-all-following-segment-delay-source-control`, `sync-all-cross-image-segment-precedence`.

**Oracle:** SYNC ALL effects are cross-image. This packet deliberately does not claim that a
single-image SYNC ALL proves synchronization or segment ordering.

**Oracle limitation:** This source-only catalogue records pending plans only. The specified synchronization,
segment delay, segment precedence, or cross-image memory-order effect is not approved as
an executable oracle in the current single-image suite: a one-image run would be vacuous
and multi-image timing, scheduling, interleaving, progress, deadlock, race, or failure
observations are not portable evidence.

**Dependencies:** 11.7.2 segments in this packet; 11.7.11 STAT=/ERRMSG= NOT YET REGISTERED; 16
