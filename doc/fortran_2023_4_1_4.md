Draft catalogue: `doc/catalogues/syntax_interpretation.json`. Source: pinned

Four base units and twelve fine units separate document conventions from
the genuine source-statement restriction. `S4.1.4-001` preserves statement
boundaries, optional labels, and the embedded IF/WHERE qualification.
Source-form continuation and comments still determine the actual boundary;
INCLUDE lines are not Fortran statements.

One free-form positive control supplies five finite admission facets,
not five independent executions or five full effects. The complete
statement-context/source-form census remains pending and needs independent

capability is inferred from the control.

The ordering and expression-hierarchy references retain R502 and R1023
ownership. The `-spec` convention is expressly nonexclusive, and the
`-selector` convention does not override R722's explicit asterisk form.

### S4.1.4-001: Source-statement boundaries and embedded-statement qualification

**Source:** 4.1.4 p1; J3/24-007 PDF 45, printed 31, lines 22-25. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** A syntax term ending in -stmt follows the applicable source-form statement rules,
including an end-of-line or semicolon boundary. A statement may carry a label except
when it is part of another statement, as in the action of an IF statement or the
assignment of a WHERE statement. The embedded action is not a separately labelable
source statement. This convention is applied with the actual source-form continuation,
comment, termination and label-position rules; it is not a claim that every physical
newline ends a continued statement.

**Diagnostic obligation:** required.

**Facets:** `newline-control`, `semicolon-control`, `standalone-label-control`, `embedded-if-control`, `embedded-where-control`, `statement-context-census`.

**Oracle:** S4_1_4_001_valid is one small free-form positive control. Ordinary assignments use
newline and semicolon boundaries, an outer IF carries a legal label, and its assignment
has no independent label. A WHERE statement similarly contains an unlabeled assignment
with a defined one-element logical mask and same-shaped integer destination. Literal
checks require scalar value 3 and array element 4, only to establish that these admitted
statements are used.

**Oracle limitation:** Five finite admission facets share one program; they are not five independent executions
or full effects. No fixed-form, all-statement, universal reporting, or comprehensive
IF/WHERE semantics claim is made. No malformed embedded-label twin is manufactured.
Canonical source-form tests retain their owners, and the complete statement-context
census remains pending. Source-form violations engage 4.2 p2(5), which requires
reporting capability, not mandatory fatal rejection.

**Dependencies:** 6.3.2.4-.6 (PDF 72) governs the free-form control. 6.3.3.3-.5 (PDF 73) qualifies the
general convention for fixed columns, continuation and labels. R515 (PDF 55-56),
R1141/C1148/p2 (11.1.8.4, PDF 219), and R1042/R1046/R1047 plus 10.2.3.1 p2 and 10.2.3.2
p1/p12 (PDF 197-199) establish the embedded forms, equal array shapes and checked
values. 6.2.5/R611/C603 permits the short nonzero label. Default integer values are
small, and all data are defined before use. 6.4 p3 (PDF 73) explicitly keeps INCLUDE
lines outside Fortran statements.
