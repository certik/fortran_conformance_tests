### S11.1.7.1-001: DO constructs specify repeated execution of executable constructs called loops

**Source:** 11.1.7.1 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p210. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The DO construct specifies repeated execution of a sequence of executable constructs,
and such a repeated sequence is called a loop.

### S11.1.7.1-002: DO loops include count-determined, indefinite, and DO CONCURRENT forms with EXIT and CYCLE dependencies

**Source:** 11.1.7.1 p2; J3/24-007 PDF p210. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** The number of loop iterations can be determined at the beginning of DO execution, or
left indefinite as DO forever or DO WHILE. DO CONCURRENT may leave iteration execution
order indeterminate. Immediate loop termination and iteration curtailment are
dependencies on EXIT and CYCLE rules.
