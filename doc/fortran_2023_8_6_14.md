### C893: An omitted-list SAVE excludes other explicit SAVE appearances in its scope

**Source:** 8.6.14 C893, physical PDF140. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** If a SAVE statement with no saved entity list appears in a scoping unit, no other
appearance of a SAVE attr-spec or SAVE statement is permitted in that same scoping unit.
This includes another bare or listed SAVE and the type-declaration SAVE route. It is a
scope-wide syntactic restriction, not a last-statement-wins rule, a ban on implicit SAVE
or a file-wide count.

**Oracle:** Complete same-scope statement/attribute matrices with one-deletion controls, distinct

**Source:** 8.6.14 C894, physical PDF140. **Class:** Restriction.

**Definition:** A proc-pointer-name denotes a procedure pointer. Naming an ordinary external, module,
internal or dummy procedure does not give it the POINTER attribute. The separate

**Oracle limitation:** Data-pointer and procedure-pointer roles, ordinary procedures and function results are
