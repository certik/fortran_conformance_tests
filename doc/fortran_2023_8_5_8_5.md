Single-star, explicit scalar-prefix and multi-star forms are different
productions with different declaration roles. RANK(*) associates have their
own expressly defined assumed-size, rank-one context. Empty data does not
make these star forms scalar, and a RANK clause is not an assumed-size
declaration substitute.

Conceptual sequence size is not an ordinary whole SIZE/SHAPE result.
Known prefix extents and the last lower bound do not supply a last upper
bound. An explicitly bounded section can have a shape without supplying
the parent's missing extent. Forwarded assumed-rank inquiry sentinels are
not recovered size; TYPE(*) forwarding also retains C716's extra restriction.

not assumed bytes or encodings. For a nonzero dummy length, partial trailing
groups are excluded. Zero dummy length instead has the processor-maximum
element-count rule in 15.5.2.12p5-p6; there is no division-by-zero test or
