### S8.10.2.4-001: COMMON aligns the specified named and blank storage views

share their first unit; zero-sized blank blocks associate mutually and with that first
nonzero blank unit. Subsequent unit alignment, definition status and valid observations
retain 19.5.3 and19.6; this is not an address-identity claim.
