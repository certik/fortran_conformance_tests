### S9.5.3.2-001: A multiple subscript expands to a sequence of consecutive dimension subscripts

**Source:** 9.5.3.2 p1, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF155. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A multiple-subscript specifies a sequence of subscripts whose length is the size of the
multiple-subscript expression. The effect is as though the individual values in that
sequence appeared as ordinary subscripts for consecutive dimensions, without the @
marker. The rank-one, integer-expression, and section-subscript grammar restrictions are
owned by 9.5.3.1.

### S9.5.3.2-002: Omitted multiple subscript triplet expressions use lower bounds, upper bounds, and stride one

**Source:** 9.5.3.2 p2, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF155. **Class:** Effect.

array whose element values are the lower bounds of the corresponding dimensions, an

### S9.5.3.2-003: A multiple subscript triplet expands to conforming subscript triplets with scalar broadcast

**Source:** 9.5.3.2 p3, J3/24-007, 18 December 2023, physical PDF155-PDF156. **Class:** Effect.

**Definition:** A multiple-subscript-triplet specifies a sequence of subscript triplets whose count is
the size of one of its array int-exprs. Any scalar int-expr acts as though broadcast to
the shape of an array int-expr. Corresponding elements of the first, second, and third
effective arrays act respectively as the first subscript, second subscript, and stride
of each generated subscript triplet.
