### S10.1.2.8-001: Level-5 expressions are the logical-operator layers over level-4 expressions

**Source:** 10.1.2.8 p1; J3/24-007 PDF p171. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** Level-5 expressions are level-4 expressions optionally involving .NOT., .AND., .OR.,
.EQV., and .NEQV. The grammar layers not before level-4, then and, then or, then
equivalence.

**Diagnostic obligation:** not-required.

**Facets:** `level-4-base`, `not-layer`, `and-layer`, `or-layer`, `equivalence-layer`.

**Oracle:** Future controls must use logical operands with literal truth tables and must not claim
evaluation short-circuit behavior beyond later evaluation rules.

### R1015: An and-operand is an optional .NOT. followed by a level-4 expression

**Source:** 10.1.2.8 R1015; J3/24-007 PDF p171. **Class:** Syntax.

**Definition:** The and-operand syntax is an optional not-op followed by level-4-expr. A second

### R1018: A level-5 expression is a left-recursive chain of equiv-operands joined by .EQV. or .NEQV.

**Definition:** The level-5-expr syntax is either an equiv-operand or a level-5-expr followed by
